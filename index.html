INT 335 • Design Thinking Project

Solving "The Empty Bottle Problem"

Across large college campuses and public complexes, finding clean drinking water often means walking blindly from floor to floor with an empty bottle—only to discover dry coolers or unhygienic taps.

01

The Problem

Students and visitors waste valuable time searching unfamiliar buildings for water stations without knowing which units actually have water or are properly maintained.

02

How AquaFind Helps

AquaFind combines browser geolocation, Haversine 50–100 metre radius filtering, OpenStreetMap navigation, and clear availability & cleanliness indicators in one interface.

03

Key Benefits

Faster hydration between classes, fewer single-use plastic bottle purchases, transparent hygiene visibility, and community issue logging for maintenance teams.

Prototype Scope & Cleanliness Note: AquaFind's current release uses simulated demonstration dispenser data around a fictional campus. Cleanliness scores reflect physical surface inspection indicators rather than laboratory or microbiological water-safety testing.
